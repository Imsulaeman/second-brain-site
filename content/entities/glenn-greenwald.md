# Glenn Greenwald

Glenn Greenwald is a journalist and privacy advocate whose work in this wiki centers on the political and human meaning of surveillance after the Snowden revelations.

He matters here less as a full biographical figure than as a forceful articulator of why [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] is tied to freedom, dissent, and nonconformity rather than to secrecy-for-crime alone.
