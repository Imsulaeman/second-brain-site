# Daniel Levitin

Neuroscientist and author. His TED talk [[sources/how-to-stay-calm-under-stress-daniel-levitin|How to Stay Calm When You Know You'll Be Stressed]] translates stress neuroscience and [[entities/Daniel-Kahneman|Danny Kahneman]]'s prospective hindsight practice into personal systems — designated places for essentials, travel document backups, and pre-mortem questions for medical decisions including [[concepts/number-needed-to-treat|NNT]] statistics.

The Montreal lockout story (broken window → forgotten passport → eight hours by the lavatory) is the narrative spine: cortisol-impaired thinking compounds small failures into catastrophes.
