# Naval Ravikant

Naval Ravikant is an entrepreneur, investor, and public thinker associated with AngelList, startups, investing, wealth creation, clear thinking, and happiness. In this wiki, he is currently represented through [[sources/the-almanack-of-naval-ravikant]], a curated collection by Eric Jorgenson.

---

## Core Ideas

- **Wealth is assets that earn while you sleep**, not money or status.
- **Productize yourself:** combine authenticity, specific knowledge, leverage, and accountability.
- **Specific knowledge** is found through curiosity and obsession, not generic training.
- **Permissionless leverage** through code and media lets individuals scale without needing capital or labor permission.
- **Judgment is underrated** because leverage magnifies small differences in decision quality.
- **Long-term games with long-term people** create compounding returns in wealth, relationships, and reputation.
- **Happiness is learned**, not earned automatically through external success.
- **Desire should be chosen carefully** because desire creates suffering until resolved.
