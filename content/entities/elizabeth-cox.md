# Elizabeth Cox

Elizabeth Cox is the author of a reasoning / fallacy teaching series built around historical and legal case studies. Her pieces in this wiki focus less on abstract formal logic and more on **how fallacies distort real decisions in medicine, politics, law, and climate discourse**.

---

## Distinctive Contribution

Her format is consistent:

- pick one named fallacy
- attach it to a memorable real-world case
- explain the reasoning error in plain language
- generalize the lesson into modern critical-thinking practice

This makes her work useful as an accessible bridge between abstract logic and applied judgment.
