# Edward Snowden

Edward Snowden is the whistleblower whose disclosures about mass surveillance pushed privacy, state monitoring, and digital freedom into global public debate.

In this wiki he functions mainly as the historical trigger behind the privacy and surveillance arguments surfaced in [[sources/glenn-greenwald-why-privacy-matters]].
