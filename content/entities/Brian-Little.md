# Brian Little

Personality psychologist; researcher spanning "neurons to narratives." Former Harvard professor; featured in Susan Cain's *Quiet*. Describes himself as an extreme introvert who acts extroverted as a core professional project.

His TED talk [[sources/who-are-you-really-brian-little|Who Are You, Really?]] introduces [[concepts/free-traits|free traits]], [[concepts/personal-projects|personal projects]], and the [[concepts/three-natures-of-personality|three natures]] frame alongside the [[concepts/big-five-personality|Big Five]].
