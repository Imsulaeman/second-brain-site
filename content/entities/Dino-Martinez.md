# Dino Martinez

Assistant Director of the UAB Career Center; 35 years across seven college campuses in career services, leadership development, residence life, and related roles. Creator of the MMI Bootcamp (mock multiple-mini interview format for medical and professional school admissions). Instructor for UNIV 200: Job Search Essentials and UNIV 300: Career Mapping.

His TEDxUAB talk [[sources/telling-stories-to-improve-interview-success-dino-martinez|Telling Stories to Improve Interview Success]] teaches [[concepts/interview-storytelling|story-based interviewing]] via the [[concepts/star-method|STAR method]], [[concepts/present-past-future-introduction|present/past/future introductions]], and self-research before behavioral questions.
