# Josh Waitzkin

Josh Waitzkin is a chess master and martial artist used in this wiki as an example of cross-domain skill transfer. In *[[sources/the-power-of-belief-mindset-and-success]]*, [[entities/eduardo-briceno|Eduardo Briceno]] presents him as someone who did not just master one field, but learned how to improve in a way that could be reapplied elsewhere.

What matters here is not his biography in full. It is the role he plays in the argument for [[concepts/growth-mindset|growth mindset]]: the idea that ability is not merely discovered but built, and that the methods of growth can travel across domains.
