# Eduardo Briceno

Eduardo Briceno is a speaker and educator best known here for extending [[concepts/growth-mindset|growth mindset]] from classroom psychology into a broader theory of success, learning, and performance. In *[[sources/the-power-of-belief-mindset-and-success]]*, he argues that effort, resilience, and persistence are not the root variable. They are often consequences of how people think ability works.

His distinctive contribution in this wiki is scale. [[entities/carol-dweck|Carol Dweck]]'s framing begins with students and schooling; Briceno pushes the same structure into work, negotiation, relationships, and social conflict. He is useful not because he replaces Dweck, but because he shows how portable the mindset distinction can become.
