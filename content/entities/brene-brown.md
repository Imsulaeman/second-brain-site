Brené Brown is a researcher and storyteller whose work on vulnerability, shame, and empathy has shaped how many fields think about human connection.

Her RSA Animate video on empathy versus sympathy, drawing on [[entities/theresa-wiseman|Theresa Wiseman]]'s four qualities, has become one of the most widely referenced explanations of the distinction between feeling with someone and feeling for them from a distance.

In the wiki she anchors the core definition and the warning against silver-lining responses that block real empathy.
