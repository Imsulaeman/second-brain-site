# Laura Klein

UX designer, product strategist, and author of *Build Better Products* and *UX for Lean Startups*. Known for practical, no-nonsense takes on how design and engineering teams should work together in agile environments.

In the wiki she contributes the Done Column anti-pattern: teams that ship features and move them to Done without ever returning to improve them, even when those features are incomplete or unused. Her 2019–2020 research with agile teams found this was one of the most common failures — and one of the least agile things a team can do. She also explains cross-functional team structure and why silos produce waterfall outcomes even in ostensibly agile organizations.
