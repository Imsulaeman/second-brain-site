# cobie

cobie (pseudonym, real name Jordan Fish) is one of the most followed accounts in crypto Twitter, known for sharp market commentary, dark humor, and a long track record of public calls. Co-hosts the UpOnly podcast with Ledger. His public persona is deliberately anti-hype — consistently skeptical of narratives at peak saturation.

His role in the GCR/Do Kwon LUNA bet (2022): when [[entities/GCR]] proposed a $10M public wager that LUNA would be lower within a year and [[entities/Do-Kwon]] accepted, cobie held the escrow. The bet became one of the most documented public trades in crypto history. LUNA collapsed to near-zero in May 2022; GCR covered his position at $0.72.
