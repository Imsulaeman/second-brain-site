# Ionica Smeets

Ionica Smeets is a Dutch mathematician and science communicator whose work, in this wiki, matters as an example of teaching statistical and logical caution through vivid everyday cases rather than formal theory first.

In *The Danger of Mixing Up Causality and Correlation*, she uses jokes, media examples, and one stronger educational-policy case to separate three mistakes people often collapse into one: hidden third variables, reversed causality, and causal claims made too quickly from observational data alone. The talk is useful because it gives the abstract warning "correlation is not causation" a more diagnostic shape.
