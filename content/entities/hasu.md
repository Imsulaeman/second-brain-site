# Hasu

Pseudonymous crypto researcher, investor, and writer. Co-host of the Uncommon Core podcast alongside [[entities/su-zhu|Su Zhu]]. Known for writing on Bitcoin trust-minimization properties, the role of narratives in crypto markets, and macro crypto positioning.

In [[sources/art-of-trading-with-light-su-zhu-and-hasu]], Hasu plays the role of interlocutor: drawing out Light's trading process framework and Su Zhu's macro views, contributing his own poker background, and pushing on framing where useful. His question — "I don't have a feedback loop yet where I can make a decision that is closed in itself and know if it was good or bad" — sets up one of the interview's most productive sections on process discipline, trade journals, and decision quality.
