# Hasu

Hasu is a crypto researcher, writer, and interviewer who appears in this wiki primarily as the host and framing intelligence in [[sources/art-of-trading-with-light-su-zhu-and-hasu]].

His contribution in that source is not only to ask questions. He helps surface the conceptual structure of the discussion: poker versus trading, process versus outcome, narrative versus valuation, and Bitcoin's possible status as a shelling point. In that sense he functions as a translator between practitioner intuition and explicit reasoning.
