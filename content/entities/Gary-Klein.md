# Gary Klein

Psychologist and researcher on naturalistic decision making. Originator of the **[[concepts/premortem|pre-mortem]]** (also called prospective hindsight): before committing to a plan, imagine it failed and work backward to identify preventable causes.

[[entities/Daniel-Kahneman|Daniel Kahneman]] endorsed the technique in *Thinking, Fast and Slow* as an organizational antidote to planning optimism. [[entities/Daniel-Levitin|Daniel Levitin]] learned it from Kahneman over dinner and extended it to personal life — medical decisions, travel prep, home organization.

Kahneman's collaboration with Klein also produced the [[concepts/kind-vs-wicked-learning-environments|kind vs wicked learning environments]] framework.
