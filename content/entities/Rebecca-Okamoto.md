# Rebecca Okamoto

Communication and personal brand consultant; founder of Evoke Strategy Group. Former Procter & Gamble supply chain leader — first Asian American woman to lead supply chains in the US and Asia. Coaches managers to present complex ideas to senior stakeholders.

Her TEDxNorthwesternU talk [[sources/how-to-introduce-yourself-rebecca-okamoto|How to Introduce Yourself — and Get Hired]] argues that a [[concepts/twenty-word-introduction|20-word, listener-oriented introduction]] can outperform a credential-heavy elevator pitch — drawn from her own failed-then-fixed interview experience.
