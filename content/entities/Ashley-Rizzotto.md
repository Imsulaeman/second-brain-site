# Ashley Rizzotto

Career advisor at Nova Southeastern University's Office of Career Development. Stony Brook sociology BA; UNC Greensboro M.Ed. in Student Affairs Administration. Brooklyn native.

Her TEDxNSU talk [[sources/mastering-the-art-of-the-interview-ashley-rizzotto|Mastering the Art of the Interview]] draws on her own first-job burnout — nailing the interview by telling employers what they wanted to hear, then leaving after ten months — to teach three strategies: [[concepts/interview-non-negotiables|non-negotiables]], [[concepts/career-strengths-identification|strengths identification]], and [[concepts/alignment-through-research|alignment through research]].
