# Charles Munger

Vice Chairman of Berkshire Hathaway (1978–2023). Warren Buffett's partner for over five decades. Lawyer turned investor turned polymath thinker. Died November 28, 2023, age 99.

---

## Key Ideas

- **[[concepts/mental-models-latticework]]** — build a lattice of ~100 big ideas from all major disciplines; use them as a checklist
- **[[concepts/psychology-of-human-misjudgment]]** — 25 standard causes of cognitive error, emphasizing the [[concepts/lollapalooza-effect|lollapalooza effect]] when tendencies combine
- **[[concepts/inversion]]** — solve problems backward; ask what would cause failure, then avoid it
- **[[concepts/circle-of-competence]]** — know the boundary of what you actually understand
- **[[concepts/incentive-superpower]]** — never stop thinking about incentive effects
- **[[concepts/seamless-web-of-trust]]** — deserved trust as the highest form of organization
- Lifetime learning as moral duty

---

## Intellectual Lineage

Munger draws explicitly from: Benjamin Franklin (self-improvement, practical wisdom), Charles Darwin (disconfirming evidence method), Jacobi (inversion), Epictetus and Marcus Aurelius (stoic resilience), Cicero (multidisciplinary knowledge), Robert Cialdini (psychology of influence), Adam Smith and David Ricardo (economics), B.F. Skinner (incentive power), Max Planck (limits of knowledge).

---

## Approach to Investing

Munger shifted Buffett from Graham-style cigar-butt investing toward buying wonderful businesses at fair prices. Key principles: [[concepts/sit-on-your-ass-investing|concentrated patience]], moats, [[concepts/circle-of-competence]], and the pari-mutuel analogy (the market adjusts odds like a betting system, so edge requires thinking differently from the crowd).
