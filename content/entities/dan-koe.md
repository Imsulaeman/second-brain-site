# Dan Koe

Writer, creator, and entrepreneur. Known for the newsletter *The Modern Mastery* (thedankoe.com/letters) and courses on one-person business, writing, and self-development. Has amassed millions of followers across social platforms. Author of two books.

## Background

Self-described "boring" creator who attributes his success to being able to articulate valuable ideas clearly rather than entertainment value. 6+ years of writing, with millions of newsletter subscribers and YouTube viewers.

## Key Ideas

- **The one-person business model** — structuring work around personal leverage and compounding skills
- **The inner album of greatest hits** — building 8–10 core ideas refined through thousands of iterations of writing/speaking
- **Writing as thinking** — essays as the primary tool for ordering consciousness, developing ideas, and building a body of work AI cannot replicate
- **The meaning economy** — meaning as the scarcest commodity; those who create genuine epistemic value will be disproportionately rewarded as AI floods the world with cheap content
- **Fast content vs slow content** — fast content delivers conclusions; slow content (essays) changes beliefs by requiring the ordering process

## Recurring Themes

- Articulation as a learnable skill, not an innate talent
- Writing publicly compounds like capital
- AI abstracts skills upward; judgment and taste replace execution
- The epistemic commons is being poisoned; essays may be the last form of real thinking
