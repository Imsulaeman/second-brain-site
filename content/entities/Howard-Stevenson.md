# Howard Stevenson

Howard H. Stevenson — Sarofim-Rock Professor of Business Administration Emeritus at Harvard Business School. Pioneer of entrepreneurship education at HBS; helped define entrepreneurship as a field of academic study in the 1970s when nobody cared about it. Co-founder and early manager of what became Baupost Group (left after becoming a single parent). Long-serving board member of NPR. 16 books.

Research partner: Laura Nash (joint research on success and life satisfaction, ~150 high-achiever interviews). Results: *Just Enough: Tools for Creating Success in Your Work and Life* (2004) and *Howard's Gift: Uncommon Wisdom to Inspire Your Life's Work* (2013).

He suffered sudden cardiac arrest in 2009 — roughly 1% survival odds — which sharpened his thinking about what a life well-lived actually looks like. The near-death experience prompted the *Howard's Gift* interviews with a young colleague who said "I didn't listen to enough of your advice — can I interview you?"
