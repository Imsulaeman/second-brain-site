# STAR Method

A structure for answering behavioral interview questions by telling a complete story:

- **S**ituation — what happened, context
- **T**ask — what you had to do
- **A**ction — what you did
- **R**esults — outcome plus **learning and insight**

[[entities/Dino-Martinez|Dino Martinez]] teaches STAR (and the related **CAR** method: Context, Action, Results) for "tell me about a time when…" prompts that dominate modern interviews.

## Example Spine

Chemistry lab story: peer pressure to skip lab → rushed experiment → knocked over Bunsen burner → small fire → professor feedback that rushing diagnoses could be life-or-death for future patients → lasting habit of proper technique and undivided attention.

The results section must include **what you learned**, not just that the fire was put out. That's what signals professional maturity.

## CAR Alternative

Some people combine Situation and Task into **Context**, then Action and Results — with emphasis on insights in Results. Either structure works if the learning layer is present.
