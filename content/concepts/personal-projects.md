# Personal Projects

The "doings" in your life — the pursuits that make you individually you beyond trait labels.

[[entities/Brian-Little|Brian Little]]'s idiogenic layer: you may share extroversion or neuroticism with others, but your personal project (getting a diagnosis for your kid after three hospital visits, advocating for your mom) explains behavior better than OCEAN scores alone.

## Invisible Projects

You may have a core project nobody in the room knows about — acting out of character because the project demands it. That's when [[concepts/free-traits|free traits]] appear.

## vs Trait Labels

Little is uncomfortable putting people in pigeonholes — "I don't even think pigeons belong in pigeonholes." Personality science spans neurons to narratives; traits are one layer. Personal projects are what you should ask about:

> What are your core projects in your life?
