# Plan for Victory

A **plan for victory** is a concrete, step-by-step list of actions that ends with a goal achieved. Its primary purpose is not forward projection — it is an **early-warning system**.

The most common failure mode of large projects is *not freaking out soon enough*. A detailed plan makes slippage visible early, enabling timely escalation, scope cuts, and reinforcement requests.

Two failure modes the plan must guard against:
1. **Underestimation**: teams are too cowardly to add honest "slop" to estimates; fix by padding more than feels comfortable
2. **Not checking**: the plan is written and then ignored; fix by reviewing against it frequently and treating deviations as signals

The plan is valuable precisely because it can be falsified. A vague goal or roadmap can never be "behind" — a concrete step list can.

Pairs with [[concepts/ooda-loop-for-projects]]: the plan sets the target; the OODA loop detects when you're drifting from it.
