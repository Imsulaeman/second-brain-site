# Three Natures of Personality

[[entities/Brian-Little|Brian Little]]'s frame for what makes a person:

1. **Biogenic nature** — neurophysiology; how you're wired
2. **Sociogenic nature** (second nature) — culture and social shaping
3. **Idiogenic nature** — what makes you individually you; your [[concepts/personal-projects|personal projects]]

## Why Three Layers

Trait psychology (e.g. [[concepts/big-five-personality|Big Five]]) captures biogenic and some sociogenic variance. It misses the idiogenic: Elizabeth and George may share neuroticism but have distinctively Elizabethan or Georgian behaviors that traits alone won't predict.

Little's summary: each of us is, in certain respects, **like all other people, like some other people, and like no other person.**
