# Impact, agency, and taste

After technical ability is screened, the bottleneck is leverage (impact-per-hour).

High-leverage categories include tooling improvements, promising research directions, system design, and data digging (even seemingly rote versions).

Two components:
- Agency: initiative + relentlessness/resourcefulness to make things happen and succeed.
- Taste: intuition for what will/won't work (large scale: important problems; small scale: approaches).

Without either, low leverage.

Improvement is mostly practice with reality feedback. You have permission to exercise both.

Agency practice: work backwards from the root goal rather than the assigned task.
