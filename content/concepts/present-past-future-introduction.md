# Present-Past-Future Introduction

A 2–3 minute structure for answering "tell me about yourself" in interviews: **present** (who you are professionally now) → **past** (relevant experience) → **future** (why this role/opportunity).

[[entities/Dino-Martinez|Dino Martinez]] contrasts this with [[concepts/twenty-word-introduction|20-word sound bites]] — longer form, still position-tied, still a performance of communication skill.

## Rules

- Directly related to the position, job, or school you applied for
- About 2–3 minutes total — you don't have three months to get acquainted
- Don't be five minutes in and still in fifth grade; past should be **relevant**, not biographical exhaust
- Add color after the core spine (30-second version → 2.5-minute enriched version with personality)

## Martinez Demo

Core (30 sec): "Hi, I'm Dino Martinez, Assistant Director at UAB Career Center; 35 years on seven campuses; I look forward to talking about interview storytelling tonight."

Enriched (~2.5 min): adds student populations advised, Cleveland origin, organic chemistry pivot, RA experience, career path through housing, leadership, Greek life, marketing — all tying to why he's credible on this topic.

## Soft Skills Signal

"Tell me about yourself" tests presentation: confidence, cadence, nervous energy channeled into **eagerness** (prepared, positive nerves) rather than anxiety.
