# AI Bias

AI bias is the reproduction or amplification of unfair patterns, stereotypes, or unequal treatment in model outputs.

These distortions can arise from biased training data, biased labeling, biased deployment context, or the interaction between model design and social reality. The result is that technical outputs can inherit and scale human distortions while appearing objective.
