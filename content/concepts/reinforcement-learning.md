# Reinforcement Learning

Reinforcement learning is a machine-learning approach in which a system improves by receiving feedback about the consequences of its actions over time.

Instead of learning from fixed labels, it learns through trial, response, and adjustment, gradually favoring action patterns that produce better outcomes under the chosen reward structure.
