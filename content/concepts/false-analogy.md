# False Analogy

A **false analogy** assumes that because two things are similar in one respect, they must also be similar in other important respects.

---

## Core Idea

Analogies help explain ideas, but they do not prove equivalence. The key question is whether the shared similarity is actually relevant to the conclusion.

This fallacy appears when:

- surface resemblance replaces causal analysis
- important differences are ignored
- analogy is used as a substitute for evidence
