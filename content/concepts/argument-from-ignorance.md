# Argument from Ignorance

The **argument from ignorance** treats a claim as true because it has not been disproven, or false because it has not been proven.

---

## Core Idea

The absence of refutation is not proof. This fallacy usually works by shifting the burden of proof away from the claimant and onto everyone else.

It is especially dangerous when:

- the accusation is hard to disprove
- secrecy is part of the allegation
- fear makes vague suspicion politically effective
