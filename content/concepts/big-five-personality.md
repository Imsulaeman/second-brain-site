# Big Five Personality

The dominant trait-psychology model [[entities/Brian-Little|Brian Little]] summarizes as **OCEAN** — five normally distributed dimensions describing universal human differences:

| Letter | Trait | Pole |
|--------|-------|------|
| **O** | Openness | vs closed to experience |
| **C** | Conscientiousness | vs lackadaisical |
| **E** | Extroversion | vs introversion |
| **A** | Agreeableness | vs disagreeable |
| **N** | Neuroticism | vs emotional stability |

## Life Outcomes

- **Openness + conscientiousness** predict life success through different routes — open people via audacity (occasionally oddness); conscientious via deadlines, perseverance, passion
- **Extroversion + agreeableness** predict working well with people

Traits matter for well-being and how life goes — but Little argues they are not the whole person. See [[concepts/personal-projects]] and [[concepts/free-traits]].
