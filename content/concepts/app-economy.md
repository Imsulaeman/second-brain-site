# App Economy

The app economy is the economic system built around app creation, distribution, monetization, and the much larger layers of commerce that apps facilitate.

What makes it interesting is that it has two scales at once. At the smaller scale, developers earn revenue from app sales, subscriptions, and in-app purchases. At the larger scale, apps become transaction channels for retail, transport, media, health, and services, making platforms such as the App Store into economic infrastructure rather than mere software shelves.
