# OODA Loop for Projects

OODA (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act) applied to large-scale project coordination. The key insight: most large projects are bottlenecked on **information processing**, not execution. Getting complete information is often the hard part of the project.

Running a fast OODA loop means:
- **Spending real time on it**: coordination is a primary job, not a background overhead
- **Communicating uncomfortably much**: multiple daily touchpoints in high-stakes phases; constantly seek updates from all threads
- **Tracking biggest open questions**: maintain a ranked list of top uncertainties; resolving them IS the priority list; parallelize across people
- **Reorienting frequently**: review priorities multiple times a day; parallelizing the top few items reduces switching costs when you reprioritize

The general principle: every decision in a complex project is constrained by how fast information can round-trip across the people who hold it. The project management job is to minimize that round-trip time.
