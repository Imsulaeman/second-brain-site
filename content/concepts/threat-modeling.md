# Threat Modeling

Threat modeling is the practice of deciding what you are trying to protect, from whom, how likely the threat is, what failure would cost, and how much inconvenience you are willing to accept in order to reduce that risk.

It matters because security tools only make sense inside a risk picture. Without that, people tend to overbuy complexity, underprotect real weak points, or chase abstract maximum security that does not fit their actual life.
