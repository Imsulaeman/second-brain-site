# Skills Abstracting Upward

Every major wave of technology pushes human skill up one level of abstraction. The execution layer gets automated; humans move to the layer above — judgment, direction, curation, and taste. This pattern has repeated across every significant labor-displacing technology. What changes with each wave is the timescale.

## The Historical Pattern

- **Printing press**: scribes who copied manuscripts became editors who decided what was worth printing and how to frame it
- **Industrial weaving**: hand-weavers became machine operators and quality supervisors
- **Desktop publishing**: typesetters became designers

In each case, the former skill did not disappear — it got embedded in a machine and the human role shifted upward to the decisions the machine could not make.

## What AI Is Doing Now

The same shift is happening with AI, compressed dramatically in time. The printing press spread across Europe over decades. The Industrial Revolution unfolded over a century. AI is moving faster than both, and the compound interest of early adoption is already visible month-to-month.

What AI absorbs in the current wave: research, pattern-surfacing, drafting, code generation, data processing, formatting. What it does not absorb: the judgment of what deserves to exist, originality of thought, voice, taste, and the ability to make someone see something genuinely new.

As [[entities/dan-koe]] puts it: "The difference between average and great is taste. When anyone can produce anything, choosing what deserves to exist becomes the skill."

## The Execution Trap

The failure mode is treating AI as a way to avoid labor rather than as a way to expand capacity. Using AI to skip the hard parts of a task is different from using AI to do things you could not do before. The first move saves time. The second move raises the ceiling.

For meaning-based tasks — writing that changes beliefs, creative work that requires a genuine point of view, decisions that require judgment — the struggle is where the skill forms. Outsourcing the struggle outsources the growth.

## What Moves Up

When execution abstracts away, the skills that remain scarce are:

- **Judgment** — deciding what's worth building, what's true, what matters
- **Taste** — the ability to evaluate quality without being told what good looks like
- **Agency** — acting without permission; deciding what to pursue
- **Voice** — a perspective distinct enough that it cannot be reproduced by averaging training data
- **Rhetoric and framing** — choosing how to present ideas so they land

These are not soft skills in the dismissive sense. They are the highest-value outputs of the layer that technology cannot yet touch.
