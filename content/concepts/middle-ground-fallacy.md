# Middle Ground Fallacy

The **middle ground fallacy** assumes the truth or justice of a position simply because it lies between two opposing views.

---

## Core Idea

Compromise can be prudent, but the midpoint is not automatically correct. If one side is defending something false or unjust, splitting the difference can simply preserve part of the error.

This matters most in moral disputes, where moderation can look reasonable while quietly protecting harm.
