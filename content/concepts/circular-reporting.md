# Circular Reporting

Circular reporting is a misinformation pattern in which false or weakly sourced information gets repeated across outlets until repetition itself is mistaken for verification.

The core danger is not merely error. It is the illusion of independent confirmation. Once publication B cites publication A, and later publication A cites publication B or other derivative reports, the claim can start to look socially validated even though it still traces back to one bad source.
