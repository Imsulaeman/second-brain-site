# Hippocampus

A brain structure that evolved over tens of thousands of years to track the **locations** of important things — wells, fish, fruit trees, friendly and enemy tribes.

[[entities/Daniel-Levitin|Daniel Levitin]] uses it to explain why the [[concepts/designated-place-system|designated place system]] works and why we lose moving objects.

## Spatial Memory in Practice

- **London taxicab drivers** develop enlarged hippocampi from "the Knowledge" navigation training
- **Squirrels** locate buried nuts via hippocampus — experiments disabling smell confirm they aren't using olfaction alone

The system is optimized for **static locations**. It struggles with objects that move around — keys, reading glasses, passports — which is why designating a fixed spot per item beats relying on memory of where you last set something down.
