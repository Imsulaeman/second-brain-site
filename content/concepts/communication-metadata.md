# Communication Metadata

Communication metadata is the information about a communication other than its content: who contacted whom, when, for how long, from where, using what service, and often under what subject or routing context.

Its importance is frequently underestimated because people imagine privacy as content-only. But metadata can reveal routines, relationships, health concerns, political behavior, and geographic patterns even when the message body remains unread.
