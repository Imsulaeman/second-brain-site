Stories and myths are the narratives societies tell themselves to make sense of economic arrangements and to legitimize power.

*Talking to My Daughter About the Economy* is itself structured around stories (Faust, Frankenstein, The Matrix, the POW camp, Aboriginal dispossession) precisely because Varoufakis believes that the dominant economic stories (homo economicus, the invisible hand, money as neutral) are themselves myths that obscure how power actually works. The book treats the ability to tell better stories as part of changing the economy.
