# Temptation Bundling

Temptation bundling pairs a behavior you *need* to do with a behavior you *want* to do. The formula:

> "After [HABIT I NEED], I will [HABIT I WANT]."

Or combined with [[concepts/habit-stacking|habit stacking]]:

> "After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [HABIT I NEED]. After [HABIT I NEED], I will [HABIT I WANT]."

---

## The Ronan Byrne Example

An electrical engineering student in Dublin enjoyed Netflix but knew he needed to exercise more. He hacked his stationary bike to connect it to his laptop, then wrote a program so Netflix would only run if he was pedaling above a certain speed. If he slowed down, the show paused. He had bundled watching Netflix (what he wanted) with riding the bike (what he needed). Each desire to watch became a trigger to exercise.

---

## Why It Works: Dopamine Anticipation

Dopamine spikes *before* reward, not only during it — it is the anticipation that drives action. Temptation bundling exploits this: the anticipated pleasure of the "want" activity provides motivational pull *through* the "need" activity. You don't just get a reward afterward; you anticipate it during.

This is why the sequence matters: need comes first, want follows. The want becomes the reward the need earns access to.

---

## The ABC Example

When ABC launched its 2014–2015 Thursday-night lineup (*Grey's Anatomy*, *Scandal*, *How to Get Away with Murder*), they branded the evening "TGIT on ABC" and actively encouraged viewers to make popcorn, drink red wine, and relax. They were associating watching their shows (what they needed viewers to do) with activities viewers already wanted (relax, drink wine, eat popcorn). Over time, "8 PM Thursday" became associated with comfort. The reward got associated with the cue, and the habit became attractive.

---

## Premack's Principle

The psychological backing is *Premack's Principle*: **more probable behaviors will reinforce less probable behaviors.** If you want to check social media and only permit yourself to do it after completing a workout, the more-probable behavior (social media) will over time reinforce the less-probable one (exercise). Conditioned pairing.

## The Stacked Formula in Practice

| After current habit | I will [need] | After [need] | I will [want] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning coffee | Say one thing I'm grateful for | After gratitude | Read the news |
| Back from lunch | Call three clients | After calls | Check ESPN |
| Pull out my phone | Do ten burpees | After burpees | Check social media |
