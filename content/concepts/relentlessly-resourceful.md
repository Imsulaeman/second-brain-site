# Relentlessly Resourceful

The distilled quality of a good startup founder: not merely relentless, but resourceful in the face of novel obstacles.

In interesting domains you don’t know whether the next obstacle is foam or granite. Pure determination fails; you must keep trying new things.

## Key Distinctions

- Vs. hapless: hapless is passive — letting circumstances batter you.
- Vs. general “success” recipe: in writing/painting the obstacle is often internal obtuseness (active curiosity is better); in startups obstacles are mostly external.
- Can be taught to many (especially young people still under authority) but not to everyone. Big-company and school environments do not cultivate it.

## Practical Uses

- Self-diagnosis and cofounder selection test.
- Tactical reminder: “Be relentlessly resourceful” is how you reach “Make something people want.”
- Upper bound on startup formation is the pool of people with this quality.
