# Slippery Slope Fallacy

A **slippery slope fallacy** argues that one first step will inevitably trigger a sequence ending in an extreme outcome.

---

## Core Idea

The issue is not that consequences are impossible. The issue is exaggerated certainty.

To evaluate a slope claim, ask:

- what are the actual intermediate steps?
- how likely is each one?
- what alternative paths could interrupt the chain?
