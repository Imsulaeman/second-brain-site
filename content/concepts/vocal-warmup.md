# Vocal Warmup

Six exercises [[entities/Julian-Treasure|Julian Treasure]] runs before every talk — and recommends before any important speech (stage presentation, marriage proposal, raise request, wedding toast). The voice is an engine; it doesn't perform well cold.

## The Six Exercises

1. **Sigh release** — arms up, deep breath in, sigh out (ahhhh). Repeat once.
2. **Lip ba's** — Ba ba ba ba ba ba ba ba
3. **Lip trill** — brrrrrrrrrr (like childhood motor sounds)
4. **Tongue la's** — exaggerated la la la la la la la la la
5. **Rolled R** — rrrrrrr ("champagne for the tongue")
6. **The siren** — we (high) sliding to aw (low): weeeaawww, weeeaawww. If you only have time for one, Treasure picks this.

## Why Warm Up

Warmup activates the physical apparatus — breath, lips, tongue, pitch range — so delivery choices from the [[concepts/vocal-delivery-toolbox|vocal delivery toolbox]] are available under pressure. Treasure pairs warmup with high-stakes moments where the speaker owes the audience (or the moment) their best sound.
