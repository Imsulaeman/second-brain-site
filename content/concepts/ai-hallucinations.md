# AI Hallucinations

AI hallucinations are fabricated or unsupported outputs generated by an AI system that are presented with the style of confident knowledge.

The important point is that hallucination is not just random error. It follows from systems optimized for plausible continuation rather than verified truth. That is why outputs can sound polished, specific, and sourced while still being false.
