# Sodcasting

[[entities/Julian-Treasure|Julian Treasure]]'s coinage for imposing your sound on people around you carelessly and inconsiderately — broadcasting at full volume without regard for whether others want to hear it.

It's the volume failure mode within the [[concepts/vocal-delivery-toolbox|vocal delivery toolbox]]. Some people sodcast habitually: phone calls on speaker in public, music bleeding from headphones, conversations loud enough to dominate a room.

Treasure's point isn't that volume is bad — loudness is a legitimate tool for excitement. Sodcasting is volume without judgment: no awareness of shared acoustic space.
