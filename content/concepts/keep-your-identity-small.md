# Keep Your Identity Small

Identity engagement is what turns discussions into useless or angry "religious wars." Politics and religion are common triggers because they require no expertise threshold and often lack definite answers that could provide back pressure.

The rule: you can have a fruitful discussion about a topic only if it does not engage the identities of the participants.

Practical advice: let as few things into your identity as possible. The more labels you attach to yourself, the dumber they make you.

A "scientist" identity can be an exception if treated as a placeholder (committed to following evidence, not to a fixed conclusion) rather than a substantive belief.
