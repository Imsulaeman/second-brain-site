# Introversion and Extroversion

The Big Five **E** dimension — but [[entities/Brian-Little|Brian Little]] treats it as consequential enough to unpack separately, especially because behavior can diverge from trait via [[concepts/free-traits|free traits]].

## Stimulation

Extroverts need stimulation — parties, loud environments, social magnetic cores. Introverts often seek to **reduce** stimulation (quiet upper floors at conferences). Not antisocial — may simply function better with lower input.

Internal stimulants count: caffeine works better for extroverts. Introverts on coffee doing speeded quantitative tasks may look non-quantitative — a misconstrual.

## Communication

**Extroverts:** want social encounter punctuated by closeness; stand close; lots of eye contact; rapid intimacy ("Charles" → "Charlie" → "Chuckles Baby"); prefer black-and-white concrete language.

**Introverts:** prefer contextually complex, contingent, "weasel-word" sentences — "more or less," "as it were," "not to put too fine a point upon it." Need permission for intimacy; "Charles" stays "Charles" until invited closer.

Little and extrovert colleague Tom heard the same words differently: Little said Michael "has a tendency at times of behaving in a way that some of us might see as perhaps more assertive than is normally called for." Tom: "He's an asshole!"

## Performance vs Trait

Little is an extreme introvert who acts extroverted to profess — a [[concepts/free-traits|free trait]] serving a [[concepts/personal-projects|core project]]. Requires restorative solitude afterward.
