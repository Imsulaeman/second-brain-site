# Free Traits

Moments when you transcend your trait profile to enact a script that advances a [[concepts/personal-projects|core personal project]].

[[entities/Brian-Little|Brian Little]]'s key idea: we are not just a bundle of traits. An agreeable person may act disagreeably to break through hospital administrative torpor for a sick child. An extreme introvert may act extroverted to profess — engaging students with humor at 8am because the core project (teaching, loving the field) demands it.

## Cost of Protracted Performance

Acting out of character for too long without recovery is dangerous. After pseudo-extroverted professing, Little needs restorative solitude. Susan Cain's *Quiet* chapter notes his men's-room retreats from "outrageous extroverts."

Free traits are strategic, not fake — but they require **restorative niches** afterward.

## The Better Question

Don't ask "what type are you?" Ask "what are your core projects?" Traits describe tendencies; free traits show what you will override them for.
