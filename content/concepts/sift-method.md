# SIFT Method

The SIFT method is a four-step routine for evaluating online information: Stop, Investigate the Source, Find Better Coverage, and Trace claims, quotes, and media to the original context.

Its strength is compression. Instead of asking the reader to remember dozens of evaluation rules, it gives a short sequence that works under time pressure. Each step interrupts a different failure mode: emotional impulsiveness, trust in self-description, false corroboration, and decontextualized fragments.

SIFT works especially well when paired with [[concepts/lateral-reading]] because the checklist and the browsing movement reinforce each other.
