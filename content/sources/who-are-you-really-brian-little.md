# Who Are You, Really? The Puzzle of Personality

[[entities/Brian-Little|Brian Little]]'s TED talk: personality is more than fixed traits. We are like all others, like some others, and like no other — and what makes you individually you are [[concepts/personal-projects|personal projects]] enacted through [[concepts/free-traits|free traits]].

## Trait Psychology: OCEAN

[[concepts/big-five-personality|Big Five]] dimensions (normally distributed, universal differences):

- **O**penness vs closed
- **C**onscientiousness vs lackadaisical
- **E**xtroversion vs introversion
- **A**greeableness vs disagreeable
- **N**euroticism vs stable

Openness and conscientiousness predict life success via different paths (audacious/odd vs deadlines/perseverance). Extroversion and agreeableness help working with people.

## Three Natures

[[concepts/three-natures-of-personality|Three natures]]:

1. **Biogenic** — neurophysiology
2. **Sociogenic** — culture and social context
3. **Idiogenic** — what makes you individually you

## Introversion and Extroversion

See [[concepts/introversion-extroversion]] for stimulation needs, caffeine effects, communication styles (black-and-white vs contextually complex "weasel words"), and the Tom/Brian "asshole" translation joke.

Introverts aren't necessarily antisocial — they may need to **lower stimulation** (quiet spaces, no coffee before quantitative speeded tasks).

## Free Traits and Core Projects

Traits describe tendencies; **free traits** are when you act out of character to advance a core project. Agreeable person acts disagreeably to break hospital administrative torpor for a sick child. Introverted professor acts extroverted to engage students at 8am.

Don't ask "what type are you?" Ask **"what are your core projects?"**

Protracted out-of-character performance requires **restorative niches** — repair alone after pseudo-extroversion (men's room escape, Susan Cain reference).
