A self-contained educational document that explains the difference between inductive and deductive reasoning through definitions, examples, common mistakes, and practice exercises.

The material is structured as teaching content rather than original research. It presents both forms of reasoning side by side, shows how each behaves in mathematics and real life, and includes exercises for identifying and applying each type.
