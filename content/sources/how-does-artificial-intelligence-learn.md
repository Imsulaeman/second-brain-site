# How does artificial intelligence learn - Briana Brownell

This TED-Ed explainer introduces the three broad learning modes that anchor much of machine learning: [[concepts/unsupervised-learning|unsupervised learning]], [[concepts/supervised-learning|supervised learning]], and [[concepts/reinforcement-learning|reinforcement learning]]. Its value is not technical completeness. It is conceptual orientation.

## Three Ways To Learn

The source uses a medical-data example to distinguish the modes cleanly. Unsupervised learning finds patterns without labeled answers. Supervised learning trains on known examples and adjusts against labeled outcomes. Reinforcement learning improves through feedback about which actions or sequences lead to better results over time.

## Human Guidance And Opacity

The page also makes a useful second point: even when these systems are built by people, the resulting learned strategies may not be fully transparent to their creators. As models become more self-directed and complex, interpretability becomes harder.

Worth coming back to: this source is a good map page. It does not teach the mathematics of modern AI, but it gives names to the three broad learning shapes that many later systems combine.
